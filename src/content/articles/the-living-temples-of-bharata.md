A temple in Bhārata is not only a building; it is a map of the cosmos rendered in stone.

## Designed by the śāstras

Texts on architecture, the vāstu and śilpa śāstras, set out proportions, orientation and iconography, so that the temple becomes a place where the divine is received.

## A centre of community life

For centuries temples have sustained music, dance, learning and charity, gathering the whole community around worship.
