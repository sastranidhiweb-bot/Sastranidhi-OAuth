Hurt by his stepmother’s words, the young prince Dhruva left the palace resolved to find the Lord Himself.

## Guidance on the path

Nārada Muni met the boy in the forest and, seeing his resolve, taught him the mantra and the method of meditation.

## The reward of steadfastness

Dhruva’s austerity was so intense that the Lord appeared before him. The child who had sought a kingdom found something greater, and the Pole Star bears his name.
