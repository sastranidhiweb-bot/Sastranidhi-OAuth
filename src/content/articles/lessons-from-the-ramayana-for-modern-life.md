The Rāmāyaṇa shows dharma not as an abstraction but as choices made under pressure.

## The ideal of Rāma

Rāma’s acceptance of exile to honour his father’s word remains one of literature’s great examples of integrity.

## Loyalty and service

Lakṣmaṇa, Sītā and Hanumān each show a different face of steadfast devotion.
