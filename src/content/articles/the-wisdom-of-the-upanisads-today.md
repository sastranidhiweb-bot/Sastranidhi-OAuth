The Upaniṣads ask the oldest questions: Who am I? What is real? What remains when everything changes?

## A dialogue, not a decree

Their teachings unfold as conversations between teacher and student, inviting reflection rather than mere acceptance.

> Lead me from the unreal to the real, from darkness to light. (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 1.3.28)
