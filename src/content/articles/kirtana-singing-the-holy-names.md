Kīrtana means to glorify, and in practice it most often means singing the names of God together, to the sound of mṛdaṅga drums and karatāla cymbals.

## Śrī Caitanya’s saṅkīrtana

In sixteenth-century Bengal, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu led great processions of saṅkīrtana through the streets of Navadvīpa, inviting everyone, regardless of birth or learning, to join in chanting.

> Harer nāma harer nāma harer nāmaiva kevalam (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa)

## Why sing together?

The śāstras describe hearing and chanting (śravaṇa and kīrtana) as the first of the limbs of devotion. Singing together turns that practice into a shared celebration, open to anyone who can listen.
