Kāśī, also called Vārāṇasī, is counted among the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, and has been a centre of learning and pilgrimage for millennia.

## The ghāṭas

Its long line of stone steps leads down to the Gaṅgā. At dawn, pilgrims bathe and offer prayers; at dusk, the ārati is offered to the river with lamps and conch shells.

## A city of scholars

For centuries students came to Kāśī to study Sanskrit grammar, philosophy and the Vedas, and many of the great commentaries were written or taught here.

The śāstras honour the Gaṅgā as a river that purifies all who bathe in her with faith.
