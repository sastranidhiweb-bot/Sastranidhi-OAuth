India holds one of the largest manuscript traditions in the world, on palm leaf, birch bark and paper, in many scripts and languages.

## Why preservation matters

Many works survive in only a handful of copies. Cataloguing, conservation and careful digitisation make them available to scholars and students without further wear on the originals.

## From manuscript to edition

A critical edition compares manuscripts line by line, so that readers can trust the text they study.
